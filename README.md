Say It Right - Typing Practice

This is a little typing game I made. You type a sentence on the screen and it tracks your speed and accuracy.
If you type the wrong letter, it actually says the correct letter out loud so you know what you messed up without looking on your keyboard.

It's just plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript - no frameworks, no installing anything. You can literally just open index.html in your browser and it works.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What it does
Gives you a sentence to type (it goes through a few different ones, not always the same one)
If you press the wrong key it speaks the correct letter out loud
Shows your time, WPM (words per minute), accuracy, and mistakes while you type
You can pause if you need a break, and it auto-pauses if you stop typing for too long
Gives you a score and star rating (1-3 stars) at the end of each try
Keeps track of streaks (days in a row you practiced) and gives you little achievement badges
Has a Progress tab that shows charts of your WPM and accuracy over time, plus which letters you mess up the most
You can export your history as a CSV file
Everything is saved in your own browser (localStorage), so your stats are just yours - nobody else sees them and you don't see anyone else's
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
How to use it
Hit Start
Wait for the 3-2-1 countdown
Type the sentence
If you mess up, listen for what letter it says and keep going
When you finish it shows your stats
Click the Progress tab to see how you're doing over time
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Notes / things to know
The voice for the "wrong letter" sound depends on your browser, so it might sound different on different computers
Your stats are saved in your browser, so if you clear your browsing data they'll be gone
